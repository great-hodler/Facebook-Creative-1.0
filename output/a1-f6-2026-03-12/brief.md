# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a1-f6-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A1 — Get Fully Booked
**Persona:** P2 — The Burnt-Out Solo Practitioner
**Format:** F6 — Direct Offer / Pricing
**Funnel Stage:** BoF — Bottom of Funnel
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Solo estheticians and massage therapists, 3–10 years in, working 6-day weeks, chronically undercharging, fully booked at $50 but barely covering costs — exhausted and looking for a way out of the volume trap

**Their current reality (in their words):** "I'm fully booked and still not making enough. I'm doing 8 sessions a day at $50 because that's what clients expect. I need to cut my load in half and still make the same money."

**What they desperately want:** A concrete business pivot — a new credential that justifies cutting sessions and doubling rates, with proof it actually works

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** The math is simple: 4 sessions at $180 = 8 sessions at $90. One weekend of study. One enrollment. One way out of the volume trap.

**Emotional trigger:** Relief that the math actually works + urgency to act before another fully-booked week burns out

**Proof element:** $50→$180 real pricing shift, $67 course cost, 84% discount from $990, one weekend to complete

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Price contrast / business math reveal
**Copy format:** Direct Punch (BoF — price-focused, reader is warm, ready for the offer)
**Key benefit:** Double your rate, cut your sessions in half, keep the same income — one credential change
**CTA:** "Enroll Today 👇"

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Relief + financial clarity
**Key visual:** Luxury spa interior with a price tag / math overlay — "$67 investment. $180 return per session." — warm amber light, upscale setting
**Mood/aesthetic:** Premium spa meets clear business proposition — beautiful but direct
**Visual style prefix:** Luxury spa interior photography. Warm amber ambient light. Upscale treatment room with marble surfaces. Professional atmospheric shot. Text overlays in clean white and gold typography. Bottom third COMPLETELY empty for copy placement.
