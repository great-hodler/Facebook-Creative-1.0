# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a6-f3-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A6 — Social Proof / Community
**Persona:** P5 — The Fresh Graduate
**Format:** F3 — Native Social UI / UGC
**Funnel Stage:** ToF — Top of Funnel
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Recent esthetics or massage school graduates, 0–2 years experience, struggling to build a client base and charge professional rates in a crowded market

**Their current reality (in their words):** "I just finished school but nobody knows me yet. I'm charging $40 because I'm scared to ask for more. I have the diploma but I feel like I don't have enough to back up a higher price."

**What they desperately want:** Social proof that real people at their level made the jump to premium pricing — and a credible shortcut to get there faster

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** 5,000+ professionals worldwide already made this leap. Many started exactly where you are — fresh out of school, charging $40, unsure. The credential gap is closeable in a weekend.

**Emotional trigger:** "If they could do it, so can I" — belonging to a community of successful practitioners

**Proof element:** 5,000+ trained professionals, 3 international certificates, $50→$180 pricing shift stories, Xenia Leon's track record

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Social proof statement / peer identification
**Copy format:** Direct Punch conversational (ToF — short, scrollable, iPhone-native feel)
**Key benefit:** Join 5,000+ professionals who upgraded their credential and their price point in one weekend
**CTA:** "Find Out More 👇"

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Belonging + aspiration + FOMO
**Key visual:** iPhone-native comment thread or story screenshot aesthetic — multiple professional headshots with review-style text overlays, organic social feel
**Mood/aesthetic:** Raw, authentic, peer-generated — looks like a real comment section or Instagram stories replies
**Visual style prefix:** iPhone 15 Pro camera POV. Raw UGC aesthetic. Authentic social media screenshot look. Comment thread or stories-reply layout. Real practitioner headshots (diverse, professional). Zero studio lighting. Looks genuinely user-generated, NOT an ad.
