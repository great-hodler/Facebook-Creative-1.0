# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a8-f5-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A8 — Pain Point
**Persona:** P3 — The Switcher
**Format:** F5 — Split-Screen Comparison
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Lash artist or PMU artist, physically burnt out, back pain and eye strain are daily reality, income capped by time

**Their current reality (in their words):** "I spend 2–3 hours hunched over clients for $80. My back gives out by Thursday. My optometrist told me my vision is getting worse. I love what I do but I can't keep doing it like this."

**What they desperately want:** An ergonomic, high-ticket service where they stand comfortably, work 45 minutes, and earn $180 — with clients rebooking every month.

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** Stop hunching for $80. Start earning $180 standing upright for 45 minutes. Your body deserves a career it can sustain.

**Emotional trigger:** Physical pain recognition → physical relief. "You can see exactly what the right side of this screen feels like."

**Proof element:** 45 min vs. 2–3 hr service. $180 vs. $80. Monthly rebooking vs. 6–8 week fills. Zero equipment.

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Warning & Disruptive — visual "before/after" of career trajectory.

**Key benefit:** Ergonomic 45-min $180 service with monthly repeat clients — built for bodies, not just calendars.

**CTA:** "See How It Works" 👇

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Recognition + relief — "That left side is me. That right side is possible."

**Key visual:** Split-screen. LEFT: lash artist hunched over client at closeup work, tense posture, eye strain visible. RIGHT: beauty professional standing upright, hands confidently working on a client's face in natural ergonomic position, client visibly relaxed. Labels: "2-HR LASH SET $80" vs "45-MIN OSTEO-LIFT $180".

**Mood/aesthetic:** LEFT: cold/muted, slightly dim. RIGHT: warm golden, spacious, premium.
