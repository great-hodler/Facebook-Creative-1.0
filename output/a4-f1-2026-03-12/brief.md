# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a4-f1-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A4 — Upgrade Your Skills
**Persona:** P4 — The Business Owner (spa/salon owner managing a team)
**Format:** F1 — Demographic Call-Out
**Funnel Stage:** MoF — Middle of Funnel
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Spa and salon owners managing 2–8 practitioners, looking for a differentiator that elevates their menu and justifies premium pricing — struggling with staff retention and commoditized service menus

**Their current reality (in their words):** "My team is trained but not differentiated. Every spa in my city offers the same treatments. I need something that makes clients choose us specifically, not just whoever has availability."

**What they desperately want:** A certifiable, premium method they can standardize across their team — one that creates a distinctive service worth $150–$200 that clients can't easily find elsewhere

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** Spa owners: one certification weekend trains you (or your lead practitioner) in the Osteo-Lift method. You add a $150–$180 service your competitors don't offer — backed by 3 international certificates.

**Emotional trigger:** Business urgency + competitive differentiation + staff skill investment

**Proof element:** 5,000+ professionals trained, $67 course, 3 international certificates, hands-only (no equipment investment), $50→$180 pricing

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Direct demographic call-out — "Spa owners:" / "If you run a spa:"
**Copy format:** Direct Punch (MoF — owner knows the space, wants the ROI argument fast)
**Key benefit:** Add a $180 premium service your team can deliver consistently — differentiated, certified, equipment-free
**CTA:** "Get Free Training 👇"

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Business clarity + team investment
**Key visual:** Clean professional demographic call-out — "Spa owners:" in large display text on premium background with team/business imagery
**Mood/aesthetic:** Premium business presentation — Forbes-meets-spa-industry feel
**Visual style prefix:** Premium business editorial photography. Clean professional setting. Upscale spa or studio context. Bold demographic call-out text as primary visual element. Sophisticated, high-contrast, business-focused.
