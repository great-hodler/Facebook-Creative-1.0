# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a7-f2-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A7 — Urgency / Scarcity / Timing
**Persona:** P3 — The Switcher (switching from tools/machines to hands-only method)
**Format:** F2 — Stop/Start Command
**Funnel Stage:** MoF — Middle of Funnel
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Practitioners currently invested in device-based treatments (LED, microcurrent, ultrasound, RF) who feel trapped by equipment costs, maintenance, and the reliance on machines to produce results

**Their current reality (in their words):** "I spent thousands on machines and I still need to sell packages around them. Every treatment is planned around the device. What happens if it breaks? What happens when clients stop wanting that device?"

**What they desperately want:** Independence from equipment — a method that produces better results with just their hands, that's also portable and has zero overhead

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** Stop buying machines that depreciate. Start building a skill that compounds. The Osteo-Lift method earns more than the machine with zero ongoing cost — and you own it permanently.

**Emotional trigger:** Urgency of an ongoing sunk cost that could be stopped + excitement of a better financial model

**Proof element:** $50→$180 pricing shift, zero equipment needed, $67 vs. thousands in device costs, permanent skill ownership

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Stop/Start command — direct contrast
**Copy format:** Direct Punch (MoF — reader knows the category, ready for the contrast argument)
**Key benefit:** Replace device dependency with a hands-only method that charges more, costs nothing ongoing, and produces structural results that last 3–6 months
**CTA:** "Learn the Method 👇"

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Liberation + decisiveness
**Key visual:** Pure typography two-column layout — left side "STOP" in red/dark with machine/device crossed out, right side "START" in gold with hand illustration
**Mood/aesthetic:** Bold command poster — like a motivational wall print meets a business pivot moment
**Visual style prefix:** Bold typographic command poster. Two-column layout: left dark red with STOP heading, right deep gold with START heading. Clean sans-serif typography only. Minimal iconography. No photography. High contrast. Statement design.
