# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a8-f2-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A8 — Pain Point
**Persona:** P3 — The Switcher
**Format:** F2 — Stop / Start Command
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Lash artists and PMU (permanent makeup) artists, 2–6 years experience, self-employed or booth rental

**Their current reality (in their words):** "My back is wrecked. My eyes are shot. I spend 3 hours hunched over a client for $80 and I can't do more than 4 sets a day. I love my clients but I'm destroying my body."

**What they desperately want:** A high-ticket service that doesn't wreck their posture. Something where they stand upright, work 45 minutes, and earn $180 — and clients come back every month.

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** Stop destroying your back and eyesight for $80. Start making $180 in 45 minutes with a service your body can sustain for 20 years.

**Emotional trigger:** Physical pain + career fear → relief + hope. "There IS another way."

**Proof element:** 45-minute session at $180. Zero equipment. Hands-only. Clients rebook monthly (vs. 6-8 week lash fills).

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Warning & Disruptive — "Warning: This Massage Method May Cause Fully Booked Calendars." adapted for physical pain angle.

**Key benefit:** Ergonomic 45-minute service at $180 that saves your back and eyesight.

**Objection:** "I don't have the skills for facial massage." → First-person HD videos. Master it in a weekend.

**CTA:** "See How It Works" 👇 (cold traffic)

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Pattern interrupt — recognition + relief

**Key visual:** Bold text-dominant design. Top: "STOP [destroying your back for $80]". Bottom: "START [making $180 in 45 min with your hands]".

**Mood/aesthetic:** High contrast. Dark background with bold white and gold typography. No clutter. The text IS the visual.
