## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Practitioners who have been recommending or assisting with injectables, relying on devices, or caught in the "filler culture" — and who are increasingly uncomfortable with where the industry is heading

**Their current reality (in their words):** "I've watched clients get addicted to filler. I'm either selling them something injectable or competing with it. I feel like I'm on the wrong side of something. And I'm exhausted from convincing clients that good skin needs chemicals."

**What they desperately want:** A non-invasive, all-hands method that delivers visible results — something they can be genuinely proud of, that doesn't involve needles, dissolving agents, or machine dependency

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** The face doesn't need chemicals or machines. It needs structural correction — at the fascia layer. One method. No injection risk. No device overhead. Results clients can see.

**Emotional trigger:** Moral alignment + professional pride + relief that a better path exists

**Proof element:** Hands-only, zero equipment, SMAS layer structural work, 3–6 month results, 5,000+ trained

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Pain identification + validation ("I see you")
**Copy format:** Direct Punch conversational (ToF — short scroll-friendly, iPhone native)
**Key benefit:** A hands-only structural method that proves faces don't need filler to change — and gives practitioners something to stand behind
**CTA:** "Watch the Free Demo 👇"

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Relief + professional conviction
**Key visual:** iPhone selfie-style video thumbnail look — practitioner in natural light looking directly at camera, candid, mid-sentence, with a thought-bubble or caption overlay
**Mood/aesthetic:** Practitioner-to-practitioner authenticity — like a real talk in a beauty group, not an ad
**Visual style prefix:** iPhone 15 Pro camera POV. Raw UGC aesthetic. Practitioner-to-practitioner direct camera talk. Natural window light. Mid-sentence candid expression. Caption text overlay like a TikTok. Zero ad production value — looks completely organic.
