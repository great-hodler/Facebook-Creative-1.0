# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a5-f5-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A5 — Client Transformation
**Persona:** P1 — The Skill-Upgrader (Massage Therapists, Cosmetologists)
**Format:** F5 — Split-Screen Before/After
**Funnel Stage:** ToF — Top of Funnel
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Licensed practitioners with 3–8 years experience who are technically good but have never delivered a result that made a client gasp — they want to produce visible, lasting transformations

**Their current reality (in their words):** "My clients leave relaxed and happy but I've never had one pull out their phone to photograph themselves right there. I want to deliver results that are genuinely visible."

**What they desperately want:** To see documented, real-looking before/after evidence that their peers are achieving — and to understand the specific method responsible

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** The difference between a pleasant facial and a jaw-dropping lift isn't talent — it's knowing which layer to work. One method. Visible results in 45 minutes. Still visible 3 months later.

**Emotional trigger:** Professional envy transformed into attainable aspiration — if she learned it, I can learn it

**Proof element:** Side-by-side student client results, 45-minute session duration, 3–6 month result longevity, 5,000+ trained

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Visual curiosity / proof invitation
**Copy format:** Long Story (ToF — emotional arc building to method revelation)
**Key benefit:** Learn the specific technique behind visible jaw-to-cheekbone structural change in a single 45-minute session
**CTA:** "See Student Results 👇"

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Awe + professional aspiration
**Key visual:** Side-by-side split-screen of a woman's face: left panel "Before" (natural, unlifted), right panel "After" (lifted, defined jawline, elevated cheekbones) — clinical lighting, no filters, authentic
**Mood/aesthetic:** Clean clinical documentation — like a dermatologist's patient photo, not a beauty ad
**Visual style prefix:** Clean clinical split-screen composition. Left panel labeled BEFORE, right panel labeled AFTER. Neutral studio lighting. No filters or retouching. Medical documentation aesthetic. Authentic transformation photography style.
