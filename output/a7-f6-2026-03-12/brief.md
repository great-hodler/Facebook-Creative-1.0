# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a7-f6-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A7 — Urgency / Scarcity
**Persona:** P2 — The Burnt-Out Solo Pro
**Format:** F6 — Direct Offer / Price Anchor
**Funnel Stage:** BoF — Bottom of Funnel
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Solo estheticians and beauty therapists, 3–8 years experience, hitting an income ceiling charging $50–$70 per session

**Their current reality (in their words):** "I've been thinking about upgrading my skills for months but I keep putting it off. I can't keep grinding at $50 facials — I'm exhausted. I just need to pull the trigger on something that actually works."

**What they desperately want:** Permission to stop hesitating, a clear ROI path, and a risk-free entry point to $180/session work

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** The 84% discount ends soon — this is the last chance to access the method that lets you charge $180/session before the price goes back to $990.

**Emotional trigger:** FOMO + loss aversion — what it costs to wait one more week at $50/session vs. what one session at $180 would earn back

**Proof element:** $67 vs $990 price anchor; 5,000+ pros trained; 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee; make back investment in a single session

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Warning & Disruptive
**Copy format:** Direct Punch (BoF — price-focused, urgency-driven)
**Key benefit:** Lock in $67 access before it returns to $990 — make back your investment with one session
**CTA:** "Claim 84% Off 👇"

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** Urgency + aspiration — "This is the moment to act"
**Key visual:** Luxury spa interior, warm amber lighting, premium treatment room. Bottom quarter: solid dark navy strip with large gold price + urgency text
**Mood/aesthetic:** High-end, aspirational — the life they're buying into, with the price that makes it accessible now
**Visual style prefix:** Luxury spa interior photography. Warm amber ambient light. Marble, clean linens, premium decor. Bottom third COMPLETELY empty of visual elements — solid dark color block only.
