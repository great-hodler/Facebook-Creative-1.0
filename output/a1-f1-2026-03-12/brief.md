# Creative Brief

**Creative ID:** a1-f1-2026-03-12
**Angle:** A1 — Get Fully Booked
**Persona:** P2 — The Burnt-Out Solo Pro
**Format:** F1 — Demographic Call-Out
**Date:** 2026-03-12

## Target Persona

**Who exactly:** Solo esthetician or beauty therapist, self-employed, income ceiling, terrified to raise prices

**Their current reality:** "I'm doing 10-hour days and still can't afford to take a day off. Every time I think about raising my prices I picture my clients leaving. I'm trapped."

**What they desperately want:** Fully booked at $150–$180/session with 4 days a week and a waiting list.

## Strategic Direction

**Core message:** ATTENTION ESTHETICIANS — You don't need more clients. You need the right service. One method, 45 minutes, $180. Fully booked without working more hours.

**Emotional trigger:** Direct identity call-out → income anxiety → aspiration flip.

**Proof element:** "Make your investment back with ONE 45-minute session at $180." 5,000+ pros. $67 access.

## Copy Direction

**Hook type:** Math & Income — demographic call-out format. Direct, specific, stops the right person cold.

**Key benefit:** A 45-minute $180 service that fills your calendar without adding more hours to your week.

**CTA:** "Learn More" 👇

## Visual Direction

**Dominant emotion:** "This is speaking directly to me." Identity recognition + aspiration.

**Key visual:** F1 format — solid dark navy top 40% with large white bold headline. Bottom 60%: professional esthetician performing confident facial massage on glowing client. Premium spa lighting.

**Mood/aesthetic:** Top: bold, authoritative. Bottom: warm, aspirational, premium spa tones.
