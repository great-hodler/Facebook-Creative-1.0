## Tone Constraints

**DO:**
- Be direct and specific — use exact dollar amounts and time figures
- Use "Facial Architect", "Top 1% Beauty Pros", "Osteo-Lift"
- Anti-BS language: "No expensive machines", "No gimmicks", "Zero overhead"
- Status-elevating: make the reader feel the Osteo-Lift is the smart choice

**DO NOT:**
- Soft language ("amazing", "incredible", "life-changing")

- Invent statistics not present in product-description.md
