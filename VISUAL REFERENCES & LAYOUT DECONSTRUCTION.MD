**VISUAL REFERENCES & LAYOUT DECONSTRUCTION.MD**

\*\*CRITICAL INSTRUCTION FOR AGENTS:\*\*  
\* \*\*For the Copywriter:\*\* Use the "Text Structure" rules to write copy that perfectly fits the physical space of the ad format. Do not exceed the word count.  
\* \*\*For the Art Director:\*\* Use the "Visual Layout & Nano Banana Prompting" rules to instruct the image generator. Always account for "negative space" (empty areas) where the text will be placed later by the designer.

\---

\#\# FORMAT 1: THE DEMOGRAPHIC CALL-OUT (TOP/BOTTOM SPLIT)  
\*\*Concept:\*\* A massive headline calling out the audience at the top, with an aesthetic, relatable image on the bottom half.  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* 1-2 short sentences. Must include demographic (e.g., "ATTENTION MASSAGE THERAPISTS:").  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Instruct Nano Banana Pro to create a "solid color block" or "clear negative space" in the top 40% of the image so text can be overlaid cleanly.

\#\# FORMAT 2: THE "STOP / START" COMMAND (THE PIVOT)  
\*\*Concept:\*\* A text-heavy, high-contrast image forcing the user to rethink their business model.   
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* Top Block: "STOP doing \[Old Method\] for \[Low Price\]." Bottom Block: "START doing \[New Method\] for \[High Price\]."  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Request a "minimalist, dark aesthetic background with subtle spa elements, 80% negative space for heavy text overlay."

\#\# FORMAT 3: THE NATIVE SOCIAL UI (iMESSAGE / IG STORY)  
\*\*Concept:\*\* Mimics an organic social media post or private message to drop the "ad shield".  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* Incredibly conversational (e.g., "Omg, my client literally fell asleep and woke up with a snatched jawline ✨.")  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Request "iPhone photography style, authentic, slightly imperfect lighting." Middle must be clear for fake UI text box overlay.

\#\# FORMAT 4: THE PROCESS CHECKLIST / ROADMAP  
\*\*Concept:\*\* Educational listicle ("3 steps to...", "5 reasons why...") overlaid on a process shot.  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* Headline \+ 3 to 5 short bullet points starting with an emoji.  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Close-up macro shot of facial massage. Ensure left or right half is darkened/blurred to accommodate a vertical list of text.

\#\# FORMAT 5: THE SPLIT-SCREEN COMPARISON (US VS. THEM)  
\*\*Concept:\*\* Visual representation of "Old Way" vs "New Way" or "Standard" vs "Premium".  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* Left Side Text: 2-3 words. Right Side Text: 2-3 words.  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Generate a clear 50/50 split scene (e.g., Left: basic facial with creams. Right: deep anatomical tissue work).

\#\# FORMAT 6: THE DIRECT OFFER / PRICE ANCHOR  
\*\*Concept:\*\* Bottom funnel/Retargeting. Bold display of course name, $67 price, and urgency.  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* "Face Sculptor Academy 2.0" \+ "$67 (Usually $990)" \+ "84% OFF Ends Soon ⏰".  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* "Luxurious, high-end educational composition. Leave the center or bottom third completely empty for a large price tag overlay."

\#\# FORMAT 7: THE ANATOMICAL / SCIENTIFIC DIAGRAM  
\*\*Concept:\*\* Instantly builds authority. Shows the "why" behind the method using anatomical visuals (muscles, fascia lines).  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* Scientific but accessible (e.g., "Target the SMAS layer, not just the skin. Release deep facial tension.").  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Request "highly detailed, aesthetic anatomical illustration of facial muscles and fascia, medical-grade but beautiful, with subtle glowing lines indicating lift vectors."

\#\# FORMAT 8: THE BOOKING / PAYMENT NOTIFICATION  
\*\*Concept:\*\* Triggers immense desire by showing financial success. A fake push notification over a lifestyle background.  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* Create notification text: "Stripe Payment: \+$180.00" OR "New Booking: Osteo-Lift Facial".  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Generate an aspirational background (e.g., aesthetic coffee cup, luxury spa lobby, or practitioner relaxing). Leave the center clean for a notification pop-up overlay.

\#\# FORMAT 9: BOLD TYPOGRAPHY / MAGAZINE COVER  
\*\*Concept:\*\* Huge, edge-to-edge text that takes up 80% of the graphic. The text IS the design.  
\* \*\*Copywriter Task:\*\* 1 massive, provocative statement (e.g., "HOW TO GET FULLY BOOKED AS A MASSAGE THERAPIST" or "3 REASONS YOUR CLIENTS AREN'T REBOOKING").  
\* \*\*Art Director Task:\*\* Request a "minimalist, solid pastel or dark brand color background with extremely subtle lighting gradients. The entire image must serve as a clean canvas for massive typography."
