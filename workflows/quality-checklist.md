Run this checklist before every upload. A creative must pass all 14 points.
If any point fails: fix and regenerate, or reject and restart the combo.

## Section 1 — Hook & First Impression

**[ ] 1. The hook grabs attention in under 3 seconds**
The first line of copy OR the visual headline must stop a scrolling thumb immediately.
Test: Cover everything except the first line. Would you stop scrolling?
Fail if: Starts with "Are you looking for..." or "Introducing..." or any generic opener.

**[ ] 2. The headline is specific and benefit-driven**
Check: Does the headline communicate a clear, specific outcome? Not features, not the course name.
Fail if: Contains words like "amazing", "best", "incredible" without specifics.
Pass examples: "Fill Your Calendar in 30 Days" / "Stop Doing $50 Facials" / "45-Min Hands-Only Lift"

---

## Section 2 — Message & Angle

**[ ] 3. The angle is consistent throughout the creative**
The same emotional angle must run through the image AND the copy. No disconnect.
Fail if: Image shows "income/aspirations" but copy leads with "better results".

**[ ] 4. The core promise is specific and believable**
The main claim must be grounded in real data from product-description.md.
Fail if: "Transform your career" (vague). Pass if: "Charge $180 for a 45-min session" (specific).

**[ ] 5. The copy is benefit-first, not feature-first**
Lead with the outcome, not the curriculum or the course details.
Fail if: "80+ video lessons filmed in first-person POV" in the hook.
Pass if: "Deliver jaw-dropping results in 45 minutes. With just your hands." in the hook.

---

## Section 3 — Audience Fit

**[ ] 6. The professional title appears in the image or first line**
The right person must recognize themselves immediately.
Examples: "ATTENTION MASSAGE THERAPISTS:" / "Estheticians, read this" / "Lash artists..."
Fail if: No professional title callout anywhere.

**[ ] 7. Speaks to ONE persona's specific pain or desire**
Would a Lash Artist AND a Salon Owner both feel this ad is "for them"? If yes → too generic.
Check: Does the copy use language from ONE persona's VOC (audience-research.md)?

---

## Section 4 — Conversion

**[ ] 8. The CTA is clear and specific**
One action only. No ambiguity.
Cold traffic: "Learn More" / "See How" / "Watch Now"
Warm: "Get Access" / "Join Now"
Retargeting: "Claim 84% Off — Ends Tonight" / "Get Lifetime Access Today"
Fail if: No CTA, or two CTAs, or "Click the link below" (no instruction on what happens after).

**[ ] 9. Pricing/offer is accurate**
If mentioned: "$67" (not $68, not $70). "84% Off" (not 85%). "Was $990" if anchoring.
Never: Made-up urgency dates without corresponding actual deadline.

---

## Section 5 — Creative Execution

**[ ] 10. Text in image is readable and correctly spelled**
Check every word rendered by Nano Banana Pro. AI can make text errors.
Fail if: Any misspelling, any truncated word, any unreadable text due to contrast issues.

**[ ] 11. Image quality is sharp and professional**
Fail if: Blurry elements, distorted faces or hands, obvious AI artifacts in skin or backgrounds.
Test: Would you stop on this image if you saw it in your Facebook feed?

**[ ] 12. The image feels premium — not AI-generated**
The output should look like a professional ad, not a "clearly AI" image.
Fail if: Unrealistic skin texture, extra fingers, floating text that looks pasted on.
If fail: Rewrite prompt with "photorealistic, editorial, professional photography aesthetic."

---

## Section 6 — Compliance

**[ ] 13. No Facebook Ads Policy violations**
Prohibited: Medical before/after claims ("fixes wrinkles", "removes eye bags" as medical statements)
Allowed: Technique descriptions ("Blepharo-Lift protocol that practitioners use for lifting results")
Check: Does any claim imply medical treatment? If yes, rephrase as practitioner skill.

**[ ] 14. No unsubstantiated superlatives**
"Best facial massage in the world" → FAIL (unprovable)
"Used by 3,000+ pros worldwide" → PASS (real number from product-description.md)
"Trusted by estheticians of Hollywood stars" → PASS (real claim from instructor bio)

## Common Failure Patterns (Learn from These)

| Failure | Root Cause | Fix |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Text misspelled in image | Prompt too long, text buried | Put exact text to render at top of prompt, explicitly labeled |
| Image looks too generic | No specific style direction | Add: "NOT a stock photo. Authentic, editorial aesthetic." |
| Copy too similar between A and B | Same hook type used | Check hooks-reference.md — force different categories |
| Headline over 40 chars | Not counted before submission | Count characters before finalizing |
| No persona callout | Forgot to brief it | Always include professional title callout in brief visual direction |
