**CREATIVE\_RULES.MD**

This document outlines the absolute laws for generating ad concepts, copy, and visual prompts for the Face Sculptor Academy 2.0. 

\---

\#\# 1\. THE "ANDROMEDA" STRATEGY (Concepts \> Variations)  
\* \*\*The Lego Principle:\*\* Do not create 10 versions of the same image just by changing the button color. Test entirely different CONCEPTS.   
\* \*\*Concept Diversity:\*\* A single ad set should contain: 1 "Us vs. Them" concept, 1 "Social Proof" concept, 1 "Native IG Story" concept, and 1 "Direct Offer" concept. Let the algorithm find who responds to what.

\---

\#\# 2\. TOP-PERFORMING VISUAL FORMATS  
\* \*\*Native "Organic" Formats:\*\* Ads that don't look like ads. Design concepts that mimic TikTok comment replies, Instagram Stories (using native IG fonts/stickers), or Apple iOS "Notes" app screenshots.  
\* \*\*Social Proof Overlay:\*\* A clean image of the massage process or a glowing client, with a massive, realistic-looking review/comment slapped right across the top third of the image.  
\* \*\*The 4-Grid Collage:\*\* Split the image into 4 squares (e.g., 1\. The Practitioner's hands, 2\. A bold headline, 3\. The $67 price, 4\. A glowing client result). This stops the scroll by forcing the eye to jump between elements.  
\* \*\*The Headliner:\*\* Absolute minimalism. A solid, scroll-stopping background color (gray or brand brights), the product/hands in the center, and a MASSIVE, disruptive headline at the top.  
\* \*\*Us vs. Them (Split Screen):\*\* Visual comparison. Left side: "Standard $50 Facials" (tired practitioner, superficial work). Right side: "The $180 Osteo-Lift" (Anatomical depth, happy practitioner).

\---

\#\# 3\. SKIMMABLE DESIGN & TYPOGRAPHY  
\* \*\*The 1-Second Rule:\*\* The user must understand the value proposition in 1-2 seconds. Clarity beats aesthetic beauty every time.  
\* \*\*Color Blocking:\*\* Use solid, bright background blocks behind text to create maximum contrast.   
\* \*\*Bold Highlights:\*\* If mentioning specific techniques (e.g., \*\*Myofascial Release\*\*, \*\*Buccal Massage\*\*), always use bolding or bullet points. Avoid walls of text.

\---

\#\# 4\. PSYCHOLOGICAL SALES TRIGGERS  
\* \*\*Price Anchoring:\*\* Never just say "It's $67." Anchor it to their reality: "Make your investment back with just ONE 45-minute session," or "Costs less than your monthly coffee budget."  
\* \*\*Scarcity & Urgency:\*\* Emphasize the "84% Off Exclusive Sale" and "Limited Time Offer" to push decision-delayed prospects. Use high-contrast colors (e.g., black background, white/yellow text) for special offer callouts.  
\* \*\*Authority Injection:\*\* Leverage Xenia Leon's 7 years of clinical experience and the "Trusted by 3,000+ Pros" statistic to build instant trust.
