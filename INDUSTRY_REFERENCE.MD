\*\*The Psychology:\*\* Make the big goal feel incredibly simple and inevitable by breaking it down into basic daily math.

\*\*The Psychology:\*\* Don't sell the action (dieting), sell the new identity (becoming an athlete). People buy status.  
\* \*\*Original (Fitness):\*\* "Stop exercising to lose weight. Start training like a hybrid athlete. Athletes don't diet, they fuel."
